## Rules that stay (every row)

- Consumed amounts use the past tense ("Consumed", never a present/future framing).
- No target, goal, ring or percentage shown to the user.
- Never "left" as a word for a remaining amount ("Leftovers" is a noun for the
  food, not an amount left).
- The banned-phrase list in `src/lib/pipeline/wording-check.ts` is the
  enforcement copy; the inventory mirrors it for its flagged section.
