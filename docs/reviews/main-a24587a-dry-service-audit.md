The central problem is **multiple owners of the same operation**: upload implementations, meal-refresh observers, media readers and nutrition projections each perform their own work. Sharing a helper alone will not remove those repeated requests; the operation needs one owner and reusable results.

Fix sizes below describe scope, **not time estimates**: **small** means a local change; **medium** crosses a few components or services; **large** changes a durable workflow, database contract or shared application state. Speed costs are structural findings unless a measured result is stated.
