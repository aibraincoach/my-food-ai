## Annotation limits

Expected food names are human-readable annotations based on the visible photo and its source description. Grams are rough visual portion estimates for comparison, not measured weights or nutritional ground truth. Composite dishes are annotated as dishes; the matcher may mark separately returned ingredients as invented even when they are plausible components. Captions identify dishes but do not provide weights. The report measures name agreement with this small annotation set; it cannot establish portion accuracy.
